Gum Graft Recovery: Protecting the Graft and Palate Site

Early gum graft healing depends on keeping the delicate tissue completely still while microscopic blood vessels connect into it. Most avoidable complications come from mechanical tugging—chewing crunchy food, brushing too soon, or pulling the lip down to inspect the stitches.

Practical recovery guidelines to confirm with your surgeon

  • Do not pull back the lip to look: Tension across the suture line can lift the graft away from its blood supply.
  • Keep foods cool and soft at first: Yogurt, eggs, mashed vegetables, pasta, and smoothies eaten with a spoon (avoiding straws) prevent scraping the palate or recipient gum.
  • Follow rinse instructions strictly: Offices commonly prescribe an antimicrobial rinse while brushing is paused directly over the grafted teeth.
  • Expect color changes during healing: A healing graft often turns pale white or gray-pink before maturing into normal pink tissue; always ask what looks expected versus concerning before you leave the clinic.
