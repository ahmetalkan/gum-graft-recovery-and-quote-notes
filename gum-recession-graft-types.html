Gum Graft Types: Palatal Tissue vs Donor Tissue Options

Periodontists select a grafting method based on whether the primary goal is covering an exposed root for sensitivity and aesthetics or creating a wider band of tough attached gum tissue around a thin margin.

TechniqueTissue sourceTypical clinical role
Connective-tissue graftSub-surface layer from the roof of your mouthWidely used to cover exposed roots with a natural color match
Free gingival graftSurface tissue directly from the palateOften chosen to thicken very thin gums or deepen attached tissue zones
Pedicle graftAdjacent gum tissue slid sideways from a neighboring toothPossible only when plenty of thick healthy gum already sits right next door
Donor tissue matrix (allograft)Sterilized processed human donor tissueAvoids a second surgical wound on the palate when treating multiple teeth

Ask your periodontist how your tissue thickness and the number of affected teeth influence the recommendation. Cost comparisons across approaches are organized on gum graft cost at Gum Graft Cost Guide.
