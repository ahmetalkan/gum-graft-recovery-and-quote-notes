Gum Graft Insurance and Quote Checklist

Dental insurance plans often review gum grafting closely to verify that the procedure addresses functional recession or root exposure rather than purely cosmetic gum reshaping.

Documentation to request for pre-determination

  1. Periodontal charting measurements: Millimeter measurements of recession depth and attached gingiva on each listed tooth.
  2. Intraoral photographs and radiographs: Many insurers require clear clinical photos showing exposed roots or thin tissue margins.
  3. Per-tooth vs contiguous-site CDT codes: Confirm whether the estimate bills each tooth individually or uses first-tooth plus each-additional-contiguous-tooth coding.
  4. Separate material and stent charges: Check whether donor tissue, biologic growth enhancers, or a custom palatal stent appear as extra fees outside insurance allowances.
